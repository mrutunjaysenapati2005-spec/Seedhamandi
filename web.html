The problem

The mandi has too many middlemen

Between a farmer's field and a consumer's kitchen, most produce passes through a commission agent, a wholesaler and a retailer — each adding a margin and a day of delay. The farmer carries all the spoilage risk and takes home the smallest share.

The solution

One platform, three jobs

SeedhaMandi doesn't add another layer to the chain — it replaces the layers with a platform, a shared fleet, and a forecasting engine.

The Marketplace

Farmers and FPOs list produce directly. Consumers and bulk buyers order directly. Price is set by supply and demand — not by how many hands it passes through.

The Fleet

A registry of local tractor owners, pickup and mini-truck owners, small goods vehicles, tempo operators and transport companies — idle capacity, turned into on-demand delivery.

The Engine

AI forecasts demand by crop and region, so farmers know what to plant and when to pick — and optimises pickup routes so a half-empty tempo never drives past a full one.

Shared logistics

Built on vehicles that already exist

No new fleet to buy. SeedhaMandi registers the local transport already on the road and fills its empty capacity.

Designed for the first-time user

No login form should stand between a farmer and a fair price

Every core action is one tap on a picture, not a menu of text. A farmer can list produce with a photo and a voice note for the price, in their own language.

  • Icon-first navigation — no reading required to get started
  • Voice input for listing produce and setting a price
  • Works in English, Hindi and Odia, with more languages planned
  • Large tap targets, built for an older phone and a data-saver connection
Take a photo of your produce, say the price out loud — done. No forms to fill.
